# AI Real Estate Automation Software Built on Your Existing CRM

When brokerages search for real estate automation software, what most of them want isn't another login. They want the CRM, website forms, calendars and e-signature tool they already pay for to work together, so agents stop copying details between them and no lead sits unanswered while everyone is out at showings. Clickmasters builds that: AI real estate automation on the stack your team already uses, covering the whole deal from first enquiry to past-client follow-up.

This page sits within our [automation tailored to specific sectors](/products/industry-specific-ai-automation). Our [real estate industry overview](/industries/ai-automation-for-real-estate) focuses on lead follow-up. Here we go further, through showings, offers, transaction checklists, closing and nurture, and explain how each stage is built and who approves what.

## What is real estate automation software?

Real estate automation software is a set of connected workflows and AI assistants that handle repetitive brokerage tasks, such as lead responses, showing scheduling, transaction checklists, CRM updates and past-client nurture, across the tools a team already uses. Agents and coordinators approve offers, documents and client commitments, while automation handles the routine steps.

## You probably don't need another app

Many teams assume automation means switching to a new all-in-one platform. Often it doesn't. If your CRM is Follow Up Boss, HubSpot, Salesforce or GoHighLevel, the data is already there. What's missing is the connective work: a lead form that creates the right contact, a showing request that books a calendar slot, a signed document that moves the deal to its next stage.

We build those connections as automations inside your accounts. You keep the tools your agents know, and you own the automations, accounts and documentation, paying platform and AI usage costs directly. If you're still comparing platforms, our guide on [how to choose AI automation tools for your business](/products/general-ai-automation/ai-automation-tools-for-businesses) sets out the trade-offs plainly.

## Where brokerage work slips through the cracks

- **Leads arrive from everywhere.** Portals, your website, ads, sign calls and referrals each land in a different inbox, often at night or on weekends.
- **Agents are out when enquiries come in.** A buyer asking about a listing at the weekend may wait until Monday for a reply.
- **Follow-up depends on memory.** Some leads get several touches, others get none, and the CRM rarely shows which.
- **Showings take a string of messages.** Coordinating buyers, listing agents and sellers through texts and emails takes time.
- **Transaction deadlines live in spreadsheets.** Inspection, appraisal and financing dates are tracked by hand, and documents get chased by email.
- **Past clients go quiet.** Once a deal closes, the relationship often stops, even though referrals and repeat business start there.

## How it works: one deal from enquiry to past-client nurture

Here is an example of how a single deal can move through an automated pipeline. The exact steps, rules and approval points are mapped with your team and approved before we build.

1. **Enquiry.** A buyer asks about a listing through a portal or your website. Where the lead source delivers by email, webhook or API, the enquiry is captured, a contact is created or updated in your CRM, and the buyer gets a prompt reply with listing details and a next step.
2. **Qualification and routing.** A few short questions capture timeline, financing status and areas of interest. The lead is routed to the right agent by your rules (area, price band, rotation or ownership).
3. **Showing.** The buyer picks a time from the agent's available slots. Confirmations and reminders go to everyone involved, and the CRM is updated.
4. **Offer.** When the agent marks an offer as started, a document checklist and draft package are prepared. **The agent reviews and approves everything before it goes to the client or the other side.**
5. **Transaction checklist.** Once under contract, key dates create tasks and reminders. Missing documents trigger requests, and **the transaction coordinator signs off each milestone.**
6. **Closing.** Closing tasks, client updates and final document filing are tracked in one place, with the agent approving client-facing messages.
7. **Past-client nurture.** After closing, the client moves into a nurture sequence your team approves, with check-ins on dates such as the purchase anniversary.

### Stage-by-stage approvals

| Stage | What's automated | Who approves |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiry | Capture, CRM record, first reply | Your team approves reply templates up front |
| Qualification | Questions, lead scoring by your rules, routing | Team lead sets the routing rules |
| Showing | Booking, confirmations, reminders | Agent or listing side confirms where required |
| Offer | Checklist and draft package preparation | Agent reviews and approves all offer documents |
| Transaction | Deadline tasks, document requests, status updates | Transaction coordinator signs off milestones |
| Closing and nurture | Client updates, filing, nurture sequences | Agent approves client-facing messages |

Licensing, disclosure and fair housing obligations remain with your brokerage. We build the workflows so required steps and approvals are explicit, and we confirm those requirements with you for each project.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need new real estate software to automate?

Usually not. Most brokerages already have a CRM, calendar, forms and e-signature tool, and automation connects them so data moves without manual copying. We build inside your existing accounts, where each system offers an API or integration.

### Can AI respond to property enquiries straight away?

Yes, an AI assistant can reply as soon as an enquiry is captured, whether it comes through your website chat or a lead source that delivers by email, webhook or API. It answers common questions from your listing data, collects details and books a showing or hands off to an agent. Your team approves the reply templates and the limits on what it answers.

### How do brokerages automate transaction coordination?

They turn the contract timeline into automated tasks, reminders and document requests tied to each deal in the CRM. Incoming documents are checked against a checklist, and the transaction coordinator signs off each milestone. The automation tracks the work; people make the decisions.

### What is a practical way to automate real estate follow-up?

Start with one lead source and build a consistent first reply, a short qualification step and routing rules inside your CRM. Add follow-up sequences and showing booking next, then extend to past-client nurture. Keeping agents able to step in at any point keeps follow-up personal.

### Does it work with Follow Up Boss?

Follow Up Boss is one of the real estate CRMs we work with, alongside HubSpot and Salesforce. The exact connections depend on the API and integrations available for your account and the other tools involved, which we confirm during discovery.

### Will automation replace my agents or transaction coordinator?

No. Automation handles repetitive admin such as replies, reminders, CRM updates and document requests. Agents and coordinators keep the client relationships, approve offers and documents, and handle anything that needs judgment.

### Who owns the automations?

Your brokerage owns the automations, accounts and documentation. You pay platform and AI usage costs directly to the providers.
