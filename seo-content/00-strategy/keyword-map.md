# Keyword Map and Page Briefs

**Scope:** 2 parent pages and 14 child pages under `/products/`, matching the navbar slugs already defined in `content/products/index.ts`.

**Method:** The existing site was analyzed through its live sitemaps and source code. Competitor SERPs were reviewed for healthcare, logistics, legal, custom AI software, AI integration and process optimization queries. **No keyword-volume tool was connected**, so volumes and difficulty are not quoted. Primary keywords were chosen on three grounds: (a) the term is not already owned by a live page on the site, (b) the intent fits what Clickmasters actually sells (built automation, not licensed SaaS), and (c) the term is specific enough for a small or mid-sized agency site to compete. Validate these choices in Google Search Console, Ubersuggest or Ahrefs before launch.

**Intent key:** C = commercial investigation, T = transactional, I = informational.
